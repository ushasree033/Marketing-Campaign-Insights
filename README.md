# 📊 Marketing Campaign Insights

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Marketing Campaign Analysis Dashboard** developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The dashboard analyzes marketing campaign performance, customer responses, campaign acceptance, complaints, campaign cost, revenue, and profit.

The objective of this project is to transform raw marketing data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

- Analyze marketing campaign performance
- Measure campaign acceptance and customer response
- Compare campaign cost and revenue
- Analyze campaign profit
- Study customer complaints
- Compare campaign performance by family type
- Identify successful campaigns
- Create an interactive business dashboard
- Generate actionable business insights

## Dashboard Analysis

### Campaign Acceptance
The dashboard compares the acceptance of different marketing campaigns and identifies the campaigns with higher customer acceptance.

### Cost vs Revenue
Campaign cost is compared with the revenue generated to understand the financial performance of the campaigns.

### Campaign Response
Customer responses are analyzed to measure campaign engagement and effectiveness.

### Family Type Analysis
Campaign performance is compared between families with adults at home and families with children at home.

### Response vs Complaints
Customer responses and complaints are compared to understand customer engagement and satisfaction.
